Privacy Policy – P.U.B. Idle

Effective from 9 November 2026. The game is published by the developer BARONisko (“we”). Česky níže.

What data the game stores

Your game progress (coins, levels, settings) is stored only on your device. We have no user accounts, we do not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts or photos, and we do not send your progress to any server of ours.

Ads (Google AdMob)

At your request (the “watch an ad” buttons) the mobile app can show a rewarded ad from Google AdMob. Google may process device data for this, e.g. the advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad view/click data, to show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. Users in the EU/EEA and the UK see Google's consent form at first launch; without consent only non-personalised ads are shown. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android settings (Google → Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (gems, Regular pass, No ads) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which product was bought, so it can be added to your game (and “No ads” restored after reinstalling). See Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) because it is set in a pub. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting data

You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone's settings.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page. Questions: the developer e-mail shown on the game's Google Play page.
