TITLE: P.U.B. Idle – Dive Bar Tycoon
SHORT: Idle bar tycoon: turn a shabby dive into a legendary pub for vampires & Cthulhu
FULL:
P.U.B. Idle is a relaxed idle bar tycoon about a pub that never closes. You inherit Barney's Dive – one squeaky beer tap, a few wobbly tables and a cellar nobody dares to enter – and slowly turn it into a pub they talk about even in the underworld.

Play for two minutes on the bus or leave the game for a whole day. Your staff keep pouring beer while you are away, so every time you come back there is a full cash box waiting for you.

EARN EVEN WHEN YOU ARE NOT PLAYING
• Hire waiters, cooks and managers who run the pub for you
• Collect offline earnings the moment you open the game
• Get a notification when the cash box is full or a VIP guest arrives
• Short sessions, one hand, portrait mode – made for the phone

BUILD A PUB EVERYONE TALKS ABOUT
• Tap the beer tap, cook in the kitchen, roll barrels up from the cellar and play dice in the corner
• Upgrade every station so each beer is worth more
• Decorate the pub and unlock new guests for your collection
• Grow your landlord's skill tree and choose your own way to get rich

DISCOVER STRANGE NEW PUBS
• The Cellar Tavern, full of vampires and skeletons
• The Frog's Joint, deep in the swamp
• The Temple of Cthulhu, where the strangest regulars of all are waiting
Every pub earns at the same time, so your empire keeps growing on all fronts.

DAY AND NIGHT
By day, ordinary folk pay in coins. When night falls, the monsters come out and pay in bones. Spend bones in the Ossuary on upgrades that make your whole tavern stronger.

GET FASTER WITH EVERY RUN
When growth slows down, call the health inspector. The pub closes, you start fresh, and you earn tankards – a permanent income bonus that makes every new run faster than the last. This is the heart of the idle bar tycoon: a little planning and your income grows from thousands to numbers with letters after them.

MORE THAN TAPPING
• Brew your own beer in the brewery
• Play darts (301) against the regulars for prizes
• Beat your rival in the weekly league and win the League Cup
• Roll the bone die in the Ossuary
• Break up bar brawls before your guests run away
• Serve VIP guests for big rewards
• Collect talismans, rare finds, guests and decorations

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Works offline – no internet connection needed
• Ads are optional: watch one only when you want a bonus
• Daily chest, daily tasks and a weekly challenge keep every day interesting
• Your progress is backed up automatically – no account or sign-in needed
• A short guide shows you the basics, and the in-game help explains everything else
• 12 languages

WHO IS IT FOR
If you enjoy idle games, tycoon games, bar or restaurant management and a bit of dark humour, you will feel at home here. No stress, no pressure – just a pub that keeps getting better while you live your life.

P.U.B. Idle is a pixel-art idle tycoon made by a small Czech indie developer. We read every review and keep adding new content with regular updates. If you have an idea for the pub, tell us in a review.
