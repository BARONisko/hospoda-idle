TITLE: P.U.B. Idle: Pub Tycoon
SHORT: Turn a shabby dive into a legendary pub. Serve beer to vampires and Cthulhu!
FULL:
Welcome to Barney's Dive! You start with one squeaky beer tap and a few wobbly tables – and turn this hole into a pub they'll talk about even in the underworld.

🍺 TAP, POUR, EARN
Tap the beer tap, cook in the kitchen, roll barrels up from the cellar and play dice in the corner. Every level makes each beer worth more.

👨‍🍳 HIRE STAFF – EARN WHILE YOU SLEEP
Waiters, cooks and managers keep the pub running while you're away. Come back to a full cash box.

🏚️ OPEN NEW PUBS
A Cellar Tavern full of vampires and skeletons, the Frog's Joint in the swamp – and finally the Temple of Cthulhu.

🌙 DAY AND NIGHT
By day, ordinary folk pay in coins. At night the monsters come out and pay in bones.

🧼 CALL THE HEALTH INSPECTOR
Close the pub, start fresh and earn tankards – a permanent income bonus that makes every new run faster.

🎯 MORE THAN TAPPING
• Brew your own beer in the brewery
• Play darts (301) for prizes
• Beat your rival in the weekly league
• Roll the bone die in the Ossuary
• Collect talismans, guests and decorations
• Grow your landlord's skill tree

🎁 FAIR AND RELAXED
• Works offline, no internet needed
• Ads are optional – only when you want a bonus
• Daily chest, daily and weekly tasks
• Short sessions, one hand, portrait mode
• 12 languages

A pixel-art idle tycoon from a small Czech indie developer. Pour the first beer – the regulars are waiting!
