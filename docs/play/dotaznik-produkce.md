# Dotazník pro přístup do produkce (Google Play Console)

Objeví se v Play Console po 14 dnech uzavřeného testu (Test and release → Production → Apply for production).
Odpovědi jsou anglicky, ke zkopírování. Co je v [hranatých závorkách], uprav podle skutečnosti.

## Part 1 – About your closed test

**How did you recruit users for your closed test?**
> I used a paid testing service (Testers Community) to reach a broad group of Android users on different devices and Android versions. I also invited friends and family who play mobile idle games. [Uprav, pokud jsi přátele nezval.]

**How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?**
> Easy

**Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test.**
> Testers played the game regularly over the 14-day period on many different devices and Android versions. They completed the tutorial, progressed through several pubs, used the day/night cycle, prestige ("health inspector") and the side activities such as darts and the weekly league. No crashes or functional bugs were reported. Their feedback focused on the store listing and on small quality-of-life features, which I implemented (see below).

**Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers and how you collected it.**
> Feedback was collected through the testing service's written report, an in-depth store listing (ASO) audit and direct messages. Testers found no crashes and no functional bugs. They asked for: (1) a longer, better structured Play Store description with relevant keywords, (2) a way to rate the app from inside the game, (3) a way to share the game with friends, and (4) a link to the privacy policy inside the app.

## Part 2 – About your app or game

**Who is the intended audience of your app?**
> Casual mobile players aged 13+ who enjoy idle, incremental and tycoon games with a light, humorous fantasy theme. The game is designed for short sessions, one-handed play in portrait mode, and works offline.

**Describe how your app provides value to users.**
> P.U.B. Idle is a relaxed idle bar tycoon: players build a small dive bar into a legendary pub and unlock fantasy locations (a vampire cellar, a swamp bar and a temple of Cthulhu). The pub keeps earning while the player is away, so it fits into short breaks. It offers long-term progression (prestige, skill tree, collections), side activities (brewing, darts, weekly league) and is fully playable offline. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, and it is available in 12 languages.

**How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?**
> [0 – 10K]  (pro malou indie hru bez reklamní kampaně realistické; když plánuješ propagaci, zvol 10K – 100K)

## Part 3 – Your production readiness

**What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?**
> - Added a "Rate us" button in Settings that opens the Play Store page (in addition to the existing in-app review prompt after a player milestone).
> - Added a "Share" button in Settings using the Android share sheet.
> - Added a "Privacy" button in Settings that opens the privacy policy.
> - Rewrote the store listing: a longer, scannable description with clear sections, relevant keywords and fewer emoji, in all 12 languages.
> - All new in-game texts were translated into all 12 supported languages.

**How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**
> The closed test ran for 14 days on many devices and Android versions without crashes or functional bugs. I fixed every issue and implemented the feature requests from testers, verified the release build (type checks, automated headless game tests, screen layout audits in several languages) and tested it on a real device. The privacy policy, Data safety form, content rating, ads declaration and target audience settings are complete.

**What did you do differently this time?** (jen pokud jde o opakovanou žádost)
> I collected structured feedback from a wider group of testers, prioritised the features they asked for, improved the store listing for discoverability and added in-app links to rating, sharing and the privacy policy.
